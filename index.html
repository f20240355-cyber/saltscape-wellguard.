05 MODEL AUDIT How to defend this in front of judges
01 · Sensor input
Four weeks of synthetic, seeded daily observations per well: water level (m above a local operational datum) and total dissolved solids proxy (mg/L). Each source has different trend/noise characteristics.
02 · Forecast & confidence
Robust recent-trend regression with exponentially higher weight on recent days. A deterministic residual-bootstrap scenario ensemble estimates 10th–90th percentiles and crossing frequencies. These are model scenario probabilities, not validated predictive probabilities.
03 · Messy data safeguards
Missing points are excluded from fitting. Data completeness, staleness, observation span, and residual spread feed a confidence-quality indicator. Sparse data can trigger a verification-only warning instead of false precision.
04 · Actionable relay
Threshold-risk category generates a concise SMS with location, lead time, reason and an action. Alerts can be queued offline and marked delivered in the simulation after connectivity returns. No actual phone gateway is connected.
Ethics & limits: All sources/measurements are simulated, not actual UAE wells. TDS is not a comprehensive water safety test; no single TDS threshold establishes safe drinking or livestock suitability. Critical decisions require local authorities, sampling, and appropriate testing. Numeric thresholds are illustrative operational triggers.
Backtest available for demonstration (not independently validated).
